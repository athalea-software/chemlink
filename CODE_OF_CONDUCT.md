# Code of conduct

ChemLink follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, and when someone
represents the project in public.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on
what is best for the project and the people using it. Harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other people's private
information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to
**[info@athalea.co](mailto:info@athalea.co)**. Reports are read by the
maintainers at Athalea and handled confidentially. If a report concerns one of
the people who read that inbox, they will not take part in handling it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on the response to each report using the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from
the project's spaces, depending on the severity and on whether the behaviour
is repeated.
